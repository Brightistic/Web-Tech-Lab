Fashion Glossary

High-end fashion made from high-quality materials and sewn with extreme attention to detail.
Prêt-à-Porter
Ready-to-wear clothing produced in standard sizes.
Capsule Wardrobe
A minimal collection of versatile clothing items that can be mixed and matched.
