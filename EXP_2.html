2. Fashion Glossary

Here are three common terms every fashion beginner should know:

Haute Couture
High-end fashion that is custom-crafted by hand for specific clients.
Capsule Wardrobe
A small collection of essential clothing items that do not go out of style.
Streetwear
A casual style of clothing that grew out of modern skateboard and street culture.

3. Fashion Guides & Trends

Steps to Build a Capsule Wardrobe (Ordered List)

  2. Pick neutral color items
